# Combat playability plan (round 41)

The user (after round 39): *prioritize playability; the tick system doesn't translate well to Minecraft combat.* An analysis workflow (4 code readers, 3 independent designs, 1 judge who checked the claims against the code) produced this plan. CONTENT_GUIDE rule 5 states the rule: keep OSRS numbers, adapt timing and precision for real time, mark adaptations `// playability`.

## Principles

- Keep every OSRS number: levels, XP, accuracy and max-hit formulas, max hits, prayer block amounts (100% against NPCs, 40% against players), drop rates and attack cadence (OSRS ticks x 12 MC between attack starts). Change only when a hit resolves, how it is shown, how it is input, and how lenient its window is. Mark every adaptation `// playability` and name the OSRS original (CONTENT_GUIDE rule 5).
- Two clocks. Minecraft ticks (50 ms) run everything a player feels: input, resolving a hit, projectile flight, checks for position, reach and line of sight, and redrawing markers. OSRS game ticks (0.6 s) stay for what players don't feel: regen, drain, poison, run energy, skilling, aggression tolerance and when a boss script starts an attack.
- Preserve rates, not instants. A player attack resolves no earlier than lastAttackTick plus the period of the attack that stamped it. Early clicks are buffered and fire on the exact ready tick. NPC cadence is stamped at launch, so a wind-up, telegraph or flight moves a hit later by up to about 1.2 s but never adds or removes one. Tests prove attacks-per-window equals the OSRS rate.
- Never lose input silently. Every click fires, is buffered with a visible queued marker, or tells the player why not. Every resolved hit has its own sound and splat: hit, miss, prayer-blocked or fizzled.
- Show it before it hurts. Shared real-time floors: any telegraphed NPC attack takes at least 36 MC (1.8 s) from its first visible cue. A damaging ground strike lands at least 3 game ticks after it is created, with its marker drawn on creation at the true radius and redrawn every 4 MC. Untelegraphed NPC projectiles fly clamp(dist/0.8, 10, 24) MC (at least 16 from bosses), and different styles land at least 20 MC apart on one target. NPC melee winds up for 6/8/10 MC by width. Hazards arm after 12 MC, then hit at most once per 12 MC per player.
- What you see is what hits. Area tests are horizontal with a y tolerance that matches the drawn ring. Melee reach is the horizontal gap between bounding boxes. Projectiles fizzle on walls, death, another level or leaving range. OSRS hits never knock back. Style colours are fixed everywhere: melee red 0xE03030, ranged green 0x40C040, magic blue 0x4080FF, typeless/AoE orange 0xFFA020.
- Fix chokepoints once, not encounters. The shared paths are the AttackTimer (every player attack), Mechanics (every AttackDef and module monster), OsrsMonsterEntity/OsrsAttackGoal/BossEntity (built-ins), ScriptedBoss.strike, RaidEffects.strike and BossFx.warn, and CombatHandler.isProtected (every protection-prayer read). Patch individual scripts only where they bypass these paths and are unplayable today.
- Combat inputs never sit behind a Screen. Protection prayers and quick-prayers are rebindable keys with idempotent server handling and an optimistic HUD. A voluntary overhead switch keeps the old prayer for an 8 MC grace window, if it had been on for at least 12 MC. Forced removals are never graced.
- Scopes stay independent. Each scope creates and uses its own new APIs (pure classes osrs/AttackClock, osrs/ThreatTiming, osrs/ArenaTiming and osrs/PrayerGrace; own packet channel ids; own self-test class registered with one line at its assigned GameplayChecks anchor). Shared files are split by method as each brief lists. Respect rule 6: no edits to src/main/resources or tools/.
- Tests prove two things: the numbers are unchanged (attack counts per window, identical roll paths) and a human can react (cue lead at or above the floor, marker drawn on the creation tick, prayers switched through the real toggle path some MC ticks after the cue). If you change timing, update existing self-tests and walks by advancing time to the new landing. Never bypass a timing problem by teleporting, setting prayers directly or calling hit functions directly. Real-player checks assert through hooks or splat recorders, not health, because TestContext makes the real player invulnerable.

## Verdict

I checked the strongest claims against the code and they hold:
- CombatHandler.playerMelee:129-133 silently returns on an early click. The same strict cooldown check is copied into MagicService:164/231, SpellService:395, MinigameCombat:176 and AutoRetaliate:149.
- Bows are vanilla BowItem with durability(384) (ModItems:76). playerRanged never reads or writes the attack timer.
- applyDamage hurts with osrs_hit while a private `applying` flag is set. no_knockback.json only lists osrs_typeless, and it is generated by tools/gen_worldgen.py, so it can only be fixed in code (rule 6).
- Mechanics.perform sends one particle burst. An untelegraphed ranged/magic attack draws a static trail and hits on the same tick. land() only checks mob.isAlive(). Hazards are 6 SNEEZE particles at a 3D radius of 1.0, sampled once per game tick.
- ScriptedBoss.gameTick runs tickStrikes() before onGameTick, and strike() draws nothing, so a delay-1 strike is never drawn. RaidEffects strikes only tick on game ticks.
- CombatHandler.NPC_LAST_ATTACK double-gates delayed hits (Jad's claw is dropped after his pending magic).
- Prayers can only be toggled through the Left-Alt Screen, and all three overheads are drawn as 'PM'.
- Food cooldowns are per item (ItemUses:114/181).

WINNING IDEAS (merged):

1. Player attacks: one server attack clock with a one-slot input buffer that resolves exactly on the ready tick (all three designs).
   - My synthesis on stamping: readiness = data.lastAttackTick + the period of the attack that stamped it.
   - This beats realtime's new attackReadyTick field and feedback's 'accept 2 ticks early' stamp. Both would break the nine existing writers of lastAttackTick: NightmareScript's `lastAttackTick == now` slow, ModernScripts' ice reset, and seven self-test/walk resets.
   - The buffer already absorbs jitter, so DPS stays exactly maxHit*accuracy/(speed*0.6 s).
   - Also adopted:
     - Melee moves to AttackEntityCallback, which kills the vanilla whiff, sweep and knockback.
     - Client hold-to-attack (realtime).
     - An on-screen OSRS timer, with vanilla-indicator resync and ATTACK_SPEED alignment.
     - A staff left-click casts the autocast spell; magic gets aim assist with line of sight.
     - Ranged and thrown weapons move onto the clock: hold-to-fire at full velocity, Rapid is -1, bows are unbreakable.
     - Auto-retaliate runs on the same clock every MC tick.
   - Rejected: client-side interception and re-buffering of early clicks. It is redundant with the server buffer and adds client risk.

2. Monster and Mechanics delivery:
   - Telegraphs get a real-time floor of 36 MC (Jad's 3 ticks as the template), redrawn style-coloured cues and per-style sounds.
   - Untelegraphed ranged/magic attacks become travelling projectiles judged on impact, with LOS and range fizzle. This is closer to OSRS hit delay.
   - Melee gets a short wind-up, there is no hit on the tick a monster arrives, and NPC_LAST_ATTACK is bypassed when the goal owns the cadence.
   - Melee reach is measured as the bounding-box gap; the style is rolled once per attack.
   - Hazards arm after a delay, are checked every MC tick at the same DPS, and use a horizontal radius.
   - Knockback is cancelled via LivingKnockBackEvent.
   - Rejected: a client-side ProjectileRenderer. It would need cross-scope client registration; server particles are enough.

3. Scripted boss and raid ground strikes:
   - The marker is drawn on creation and redrawn every 4 MC, there is a 3-game-tick floor, and the hit test is horizontal.
   - Warnings move to the vanilla subtitle channel (leniency), which eating messages cannot overwrite.
   - Feedback's custom cast-bar HUD is deferred: it needs a shared packet that would couple scopes.
   - Only the encounters that are impossible in real time get patched now: the Sol triple parry, Leviathan's 1-tick orbs, manticore orbs landing immediately, uncued Verzik P3, the late Hunllef/Hydra switch warning, Muspah's launch-time prayer read, Zebak's invisible gap and Ba-Ba's instant pit kill. Every other per-boss fix goes to later.

4. Prayer and feedback:
   - Rebindable protection keys, quick-prayers and an idempotent set packet.
   - One back-grace window: 8 MC for a voluntary switch, if the prayer had been on for at least 12 MC.
   - It is routed through the CombatHandler.isProtected chokepoint, which the other scopes point their helpers at.
   - Rejected: the 'hold the hit and re-check' late window. It touches every hit path; instead, B draws impact visuals 2-3 MC before resolution.
   - Rejected: a 'smart protect' key. It removes skill and needs a cross-scope registry of incoming attacks.
   - Also adopted: distinct overhead labels, an HUD overhead indicator, first-person self hitsplats with a BLOCKED splat and the attacker id, readable world splats, movement while the panel is open, clean freezes (no FOV zoom, no hop-out, OSRS immunity), and shared food and potion delays (the counterweight that keeps boss max hits meaningful).

5. Human-budget self-tests live in each scope's own check class.

DEFERRED (to 'later'):
- Vanilla-backed spawns on OSRS AI.
- Aggro leash.
- Top-damager loot.
- Retaliation stickiness.
- Opponent box and boss-bar status.
- Single-way visibility.
- Bulk migration of direct isActive(PROTECT_FROM_*) checks.
- Remaining boss patches.

Each is valuable but per-encounter or outside the four engine paths.

### player-attack-loop: Player attack loop: one OSRS attack clock with an input buffer, hold-to-attack, ranged on the clock, a truthful timer HUD

GOAL
The user says the tick system doesn't translate to Minecraft combat. On the player side the main cause is CombatHandler.playerMelee:129-133. DESIGN
1. Pure osrs/AttackClock.
- readyAt(lastAttack, periodMc) = lastAttack + periodMc
- ready(now, readyAt)
- PENDING_GRACE_MC = 10 and pendingExpired(now, readyAt)
- rangedSpeed(weaponTicks, rapid) = max(1, ticks - (rapid ? 1 : 0))
- vanillaAttackSpeedModifier(ticks) = -4 + 1/(ticks*0.6), the same formula as ModItems:67
- A small simulator for tests.

2. AttackTimer (server). The compatibility rule is load-bearing.
- Readiness = data.lastAttackTick + periodMc, where periodMc is the period of the attack that stamped lastAttackTick, stored in AttackTimer's per-UUID state.
- With no recorded period, use the current weapon speed x 12, which matches today's behaviour.
- Every existing direct writer keeps today's meaning:
  - self-tests set lastAttackTick = now - 10000 to make the player ready;
  - NightmareScript:117 pushes lastAttackTick = now + slow*12 when `lastAttackTick == now`;
  - ModernScripts:1172 sets ready a tick later.
- Always stamp lastAttackTick = the resolve tick, never an early or tolerance value, so the Nightmare `== now` check keeps working.

API:
- accept(player, kind, periodMc): stamps lastAttackTick and the period, then sends S2C channel RuneCraft.id("combat/attack_clock") with {remainingMc, periodMc, kind ordinal, queued=false}. Define the id in AttackTimer; send with ServerPlayNetworking.send, guarded by canSend for fake players.
- click(player, target, kind): if ready, resolve now. Otherwise store a single Pending{kind, targetId, queuedAt} (a newer click replaces it), send the clock with queued=true, and play a quiet UI click to that player only (playNotifySound).
- tick(player): every MC tick, called from PlayerEvents.everyMcTick for listed players. tickForSelfTest(player) does the same for fake players.
  - When ready, validate: the target is alive, not removed and on the same level.
  - Melee: player.canInteractWithEntity(target, 0.5) and hasLineOfSight.
  - Spell: within the spell range (20) and in line of sight (level.clip).
  - If valid: player.swing(MAIN_HAND, true), then the kind's resolver.
  - If not valid by readyAt + 10 MC: drop the pending attack and resend the clock.
  - Clear the pending attack when the selected hotbar slot or the main-hand item changes, the target dies, the player dies or changes level, or the player clicks another entity (replace).
- forget(uuid) on disconnect.

The period is chosen at attack time, as in OSRS:
- melee: Equipment.weaponSpeed (4 when requirements are unmet);
- spells: 5, or the powered staff's speed;
- ranged/thrown: AttackClock.rangedSpeed(EquipmentDef.attackSpeed, data.style().forRanged() == CombatStyle.RAPID).
